## Method and limits

Headings and word boundaries were compared with a supplied PDF edition. Text retains the repository's original letters and numbers except identified running page markers; spacing changes are checked with a normalized-content invariant. This avoids silently substituting a different edition's method or typo. Candidate sections must pass alignment and coverage checks. Missing source matches, uncertain boundaries, illustration references, system-dependent utility material and unresolved dependencies are held. Explicit special-pack chapter notes are added to accepted routines. This is a source cleanup, not a claim that all historical methods or success claims have been performance-tested.

The inherited page ranges are reference-01 extraction ranges; they have not all been narrowed to the split trick. Batches 03 and 04 document their verified page ranges separately. Source credit metadata is preserved; uncertain originators remain Source unknown. The PDFs themselves are not publicly hosted.
